
# UrbanSense-AI 🌍

UrbanSense-AI is a Smart City Environmental Intelligence Platform designed to monitor, analyze, and predict urban environmental risks using data-driven and AI-based methods.

The system focuses on early warning, sustainability planning, and citizen awareness by integrating environmental data from multiple sources.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Rapid urbanization has increased the risk of flooding, air pollution, waste overflow, and environmental degradation in modern cities.

UrbanSense-AI addresses these challenges by providing a centralized digital platform for:

- Environmental monitoring
- Risk prediction
- Data visualization
- Decision support

The system supports both authorities and citizens in taking preventive and corrective actions.

---

## 🌟 Key Modules

- Flood Risk Monitoring & Prediction  
- Air Quality Index (AQI) Forecasting  
- Waste Management Monitoring  
- Weather Alert System  
- Environmental Reports & Analytics  
- Admin Management Panel  
- Secure User Authentication
